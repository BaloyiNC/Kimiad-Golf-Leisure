Our Story

The Kimiad Legacy

Nestled in the heart of Moreleta Park, Pretoria, Kimiad Golf & Leisure has been a pillar of the local golf community for decades. Our name carries a promise: a place where the pressures of daily life dissolve into the rhythm of the fairway.

From our championship 18-hole layout to our popular driving range, coaching academy, and scenic walking trails, every corner of Kimiad is designed to connect you with the game you love — and the extraordinary natural beauty of the Highveld. We are proud to be more than a golf course; we are a community.
